ABOUT

A PLACE TO BELONG.

Café Nahum is an independent coffee shop in Jaro, Iloilo, created as a heartfelt tribute to the founder's late father, Jo. Inspired by the meaning of Nahum—comfort and a stone of help—it is a welcoming place where coffee, books, and quiet moments come together.

More than a café,
it's a place of comfort.

OUR MISSION

Make space for focus, comfort, and good coffee.

We offer Iloilo a peaceful study sanctuary while supporting local growers through carefully made coffee with regionally sourced beans.

OUR VISION

To be Jaro's trusted cozy work-and-coffee corner.

We hope to bring together local agricultural advocacy, creative focus, and genuine hospitality in one familiar neighborhood space.
